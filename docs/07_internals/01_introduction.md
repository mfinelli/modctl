# Introduction

You do not need to understand any of the content here to use modctl. The pages
in this section are for users who want a deeper picture of what modctl is doing
on their system: how it stores files, why it makes certain safety decisions,
and how the export bundle format is structured.

If you are looking for information on contributing to modctl or building it
from source, head to the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/mfinelli/modctl)
where the developer documentation lives.
